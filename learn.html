01. What is Sudoku?

Sudoku is a logic puzzle played on a 9×9 grid. Your mission is to fill every empty cell with numbers from 1 to 9. You don't need mathematics. You only need logic and concentration.

02. The three golden rules

Every number from 1 to 9 must appear exactly once in each of these areas:

↔

Rows

No repeated numbers in the same horizontal row.

↕

Columns

No repeated numbers in the same vertical column.

▦

3×3 Boxes

Every small box must contain numbers 1 to 9.

03. How to play?

1. Select an empty cell.
2. Choose a number from 1 to 9.
3. Check its row, column and 3×3 box.
4. If the number doesn't break any rule, keep it.
5. Complete all 81 cells to win!

04. Helpful tips

• Start with rows or boxes that already have many numbers.
• Look for numbers that can fit in only one position.
• Use Notes to remember possible numbers.
• Take your time. Sudoku is about thinking, not rushing.

💡 Pro Tip: Never guess randomly. Look for clues and eliminate impossible numbers.
